## 🚁 Aerial Drone Flight Manual (1km Radius)

### Launching the Drone
1. Navigate to the Rooftop Helipad (click **`ROOFTOP HELIPAD`** on the elevator panel or press <kbd>5</kbd>).
2. Walk over to the glowing cyan launch ring on the helipad.
3. Press **`[F]`** or click the pulsing button **`[LAUNCH RECON DRONE (1KM)]`**.

### In-Flight Maneuvers
* **Flight Pitch**: Press <kbd>W</kbd> to pitch the nose down and accelerate forward; press <kbd>S</kbd> to pitch up and decelerate/reverse.
* **Banking Roll**: Press <kbd>A</kbd> or <kbd>D</kbd> to bank into high-speed aerodynamic turns. The drone's roll horizon and artificial horizon will tilt in real time.
* **Climb & Dive**: Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> to climb high above the building (up to $280\text{m}$ AGL). Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>C</kbd> to descend.
* **Turbo Boost**: Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to engage the afterburners, accelerating up to **$137\text{ km/h}$** ($38\text{ m/s}$) to cross the 1km valley in seconds!
* **Night Searchlight**: The drone casts a high-power downward searchlight beam across the ground, illuminating cars and tree canopies.
* **Return to Pilot**: Press <kbd>R</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> anytime to land the drone and resume first-person character walking on the helipad.
