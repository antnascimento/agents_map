### 🛸 Apex-1 Recon Drone Flight Mode

When standing on the Story 5 Rooftop Helipad, approach the pulsing holographic launch ring and press <kbd>F</kbd> (or click the floating HUD button).

| Key | Maneuver | Aerodynamic Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>W</kbd> | Pitch Nose Down | Accelerates drone forward in heading direction |
| <kbd>S</kbd> | Pitch Nose Up | Airbrake / Reverse thrust |
| <kbd>A</kbd> / <kbd>D</kbd> | Bank & Roll Left / Right | Tilts chassis sideways for coordinated turns |
| <kbd>Q</kbd> / <kbd>E</kbd> | Yaw Steering | Turns heading left / right ($1.8\text{ rad/s}$) |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> | Throttle Up (Climb) | Powerful vertical lift ($+16\text{ m/s}$) |
| <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> | Throttle Down (Descend) | Controlled descent toward terrain |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> (Hold) | **TURBO BOOST** | Maximizes thrust up to **$137\text{ km/h}$** ($38\text{ m/s}$) |
| <kbd>R</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Return / Disengage Flight | Safely lands drone on helipad dock and returns to pilot |

> [!TIP]
> **Flight Stability**: Releasing vertical throttle (<kbd>Space</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>) automatically engages active hover stabilization, holding your current altitude in place.

> [!WARNING]
> **1km Geofence Alert**: If you fly further than **1000 meters** from the central complex, the drone's navigation computer activates soft pushback dampening, slowing your speed and turning you back toward the building. Signal strength drops as distance increases.

## 📡 Agent Comms Transceiver Terminal

Press <kbd>T</kbd> at any time to open the encrypted neural comms terminal. Here you can:
1. Review real-time status reports from **Agent Atlas**, **Agent Neo**, **Agent Iris**, and **Agent Scribe**.
2. Issue direct Commander Directives into the terminal input:
   * Type `fly` or `drone` to warp directly to the helipad and launch the Recon Drone.
   * Type `floor 1`, `floor 2`, `floor 3`, `floor 4`, or `floor 5` to engage elevator transit.
   * Type `helipad` or `roof` to warp to the rooftop deck.
